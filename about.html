Kabal, Swat — KPK

Who We Are

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

Khyber Degree College, situated in the scenic Kabal area of Swat, is committed to delivering quality education to the youth of the Swat Valley. Our institution blends traditional values with modern academic standards.

Affiliated with the University of Swat, we offer BS degree programs — including our newly launched Computer Science program — that prepare graduates for higher education and professional careers across Pakistan and beyond.

Mission & Vision

What Drives Us

The principles that guide everything we do at Khyber Degree College.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality higher education to the students of Swat Valley, fostering academic excellence, critical thinking, and character development in a supportive learning environment.

🔭 Our Vision

To be a leading degree college in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, recognized for producing graduates who excel academically and contribute positively to their communities and the nation.

💎 Our Values

Integrity, academic rigor, inclusivity, and community — we believe every student deserves a chance to learn in a peaceful, well-supported environment.
