Requirements

Eligibility & Documents

What you'll need to complete your application.

🎓 Minimum Marks Required

  • Matric (SSC): at least 45% marks from a recognized board
  • Intermediate (FSc/FA/ICS/ICom) or equivalent: at least 45% marks
  • Final merit is based on the aggregate formula below — higher marks improve your position on the merit list, they're not just a pass/fail cutoff

📘 Subject Requirements by Program

  • Computer Science: FSc Pre-Engineering, ICS, or Intermediate with Mathematics
  • Chemistry / Zoology / Botany: FSc Pre-Medical or Pre-Engineering
  • Mathematics: Intermediate with Mathematics
  • English / Urdu-Pashto Literature: FA, FSc, ICS, or ICom — any group accepted

📄 Required Documents

  • Matric & Intermediate certificates / DMCs
  • CNIC / Form-B (original + copies)
  • Domicile certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Migration certificate (if applicable)

📅 Key Dates

Admission forms are issued from the college office at the start of each academic session. Contact the admissions office for current deadlines and merit list announcements.

💰 Fees & Scholarships

Affordable fee structure with merit-based scholarships available for high-achieving students. Installment plans available on request. Contact the office for the current fee schedule by program.

📊 How Merit Is Calculated

Matric (SSC) marks30% weightage
Intermediate marks70% weightage
Aggregate Merit Score = (Matric % × 0.30) + (Intermediate % × 0.70)

Common Questions

Admission FAQs

Everything else applicants usually ask before enrolling.

Is there an entrance test?

No. Admission is purely merit-based on your Matric and Intermediate academic record — there is no separate entry test to sit for.

Can I apply with a compartment / supplementary result?

You may apply provisionally, but your admission is confirmed only once you clear the compartment and submit an updated result before the merit list deadline.

Is there an age limit for admission?

There is no strict upper age limit for BS admission at the college. You do need to meet the standard minimum age requirement for having completed Intermediate.

Can I transfer from another college or university?

Yes, migration is possible subject to seat availability and University of Swat equivalence/transfer policy. Bring your migration certificate and transcript to the admissions office for evaluation.

What if my Matric and Intermediate are from different boards?

That's fine — results from any board recognized by the relevant provincial or federal education authority are accepted. Equivalence certificates are required for qualifications from outside Pakistan.

Are scholarships available?

Yes, merit-based fee concessions and scholarships are available for high-achieving and financially deserving students. Ask the admissions office about current eligibility criteria and how to apply.

Is hostel or transport facility available?

The college currently operates as a day-scholar institution. Contact the office directly for the latest information on any transport arrangements for students commuting from nearby areas.

Can I change my program after admission?

Program changes may be considered within the first few weeks of the semester, subject to seat availability in the desired program and approval from the college administration.
