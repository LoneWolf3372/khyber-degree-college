Requirements

Eligibility & Documents

What you'll need to complete your application.

🎓 Eligibility

Intermediate (FSc/FA/ICS) or equivalent with minimum required marks from a recognized board. Computer Science applicants should ideally have an FSc Pre-Engineering, ICS, or equivalent background.

📄 Required Documents

  • Matric & Intermediate certificates / DMCs
  • CNIC / Form-B (original + copies)
  • Domicile certificate
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Migration certificate (if applicable)

📅 Key Dates

Admission forms are issued from the college office at the start of each academic session. Contact the admissions office for current deadlines and merit list announcements.

💰 Fees & Scholarships

Affordable fee structure with merit-based scholarships available for high-achieving students. Contact the office for the current fee schedule by program.
